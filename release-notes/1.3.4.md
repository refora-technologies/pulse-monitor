## Fixed

- On some laptops, GPU Usage and GPU Temp kept switching between "asleep" and their readings. Pulse now only shows "asleep" once the graphics card has really gone to sleep, so the tiles stay steady.
- Built-in graphics, such as Intel graphics, could briefly show "asleep" while they were drawing the screen. They now always show their readings.

## Improved

- Saving diagnostics now also lists the sensors on your PC and which one each tile uses, so problems on your hardware can be solved faster.
