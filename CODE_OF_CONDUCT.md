# Code of conduct

Pulse is for anyone who wants to see what their PC is doing, from first-time builders to
people who tune every clock, and its community should be welcoming, patient and kind. This
applies everywhere Pulse is discussed: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and anywhere else
people speak for the project.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone was new once. Answer the question that was asked, and
  assume good intentions.
- **Disagree about ideas, never about people.** Criticism of code, designs and suggestions is
  welcome when it's specific and respectful.
- **Respect privacy.** Never share someone else's personal details, and keep your own
  account names, file paths and passwords out of posts and logs.
- **Stay on topic,** and use the right place: questions in Q&A, ideas in Ideas, bugs in Issues.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning comments of any kind, including about age, body, disability,
  ethnicity, gender, nationality, religion or sexual orientation
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention
- Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic posts
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Encouraging any of the above

## What happens

Refora Technologies looks after this community. Posts, comments, code or people that don't
respect this code may be edited, hidden or removed, temporarily or for good, depending on how
serious and how repeated it is.

## Telling us about a problem

Email **reforatech@gmail.com**. Every report is read, and kept confidential. Include links to
what happened if you can.
